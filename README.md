### CHEM-000450: Sulfur mass balance during orpiment deterioration
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Quantify sulfur released from deteriorating As₂S₃ as H₂S, dissolved reduced sulfur, oxidized sulfur, and binder-retained species. Link each sulfur pathway to the corresponding arsenic transformation.
- **Status:** Broers and colleagues established two arsenic-degradation pathways in 2023 but explicitly excluded sulfur fate from their study. Earlier H₂S suggestions do not establish its yield or competing sinks.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Mobile sulfur products can alter neighboring pigments.
- **Key works:** F. T. H. Broers et al. (2023), “Two Pathways for the Degradation of Orpiment Pigment (As2S3) Found in Paintings,” [DOI: 10.1021/jacs.2c12271](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.2c12271).
- **Related:** Earth science—sulfur speciation during arsenic-sulfide weathering.
